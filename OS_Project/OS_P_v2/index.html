RR Round Robin – Metrics

PID AT BT WT TAT RT
What do these metrics mean?
WT (Waiting Time) – Time a process spends waiting in the ready queue.
TAT (Turnaround Time) – Total time from arrival to completion (TAT = CT − AT).
RT (Response Time) – Time from arrival until the CPU first responds.

Round Robin is fair by design — each process gets equal CPU slices of the quantum. It minimises starvation at the cost of higher average TAT for long processes.

PS Priority Scheduling – Metrics

PID AT BT Pr WT TAT RT
About Priority Scheduling
Non-Preemptive: Once a process starts, it runs to completion before a higher-priority arrival can take over.
Preemptive: A newly arrived higher-priority process immediately displaces the running process.

Starvation risk: Low-priority processes may wait indefinitely if high-priority ones keep arriving. A real OS uses aging to gradually boost priority over time.
